# 01 - Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive Sales Performance Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales, profit, customers, products, regions, segments, and monthly performance.

The project follows a complete data analysis workflow from raw sales data cleaning to KPI analysis, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and dashboard development.

## Business Problem

A retail business needs a clear view of its sales and profitability performance.

The objective is to analyze historical sales data and identify:

- Overall sales performance
- Profitability
- Sales trends
- High-performing products
- High-value customers
- Regional performance
- Customer segment performance
- Category performance

## Objectives

- Clean and prepare retail sales data
- Calculate important business KPIs
- Analyze sales and profit using PivotTables
- Identify high-performing products and customers
- Analyze regional and customer segment performance
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard
- Generate business insights from the analysis
